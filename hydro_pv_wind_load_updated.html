⚙ Prototype Status

This website presents a digital prototype of a hydel power plant energy-management system. It is a simulation and conceptual engineering demonstration—not a live control interface for the physical Reference Hydropower Plant. The Hydro–PV–Wind–BESS configuration is used to demonstrate how hydropower flexibility, solar and wind generation, and battery storage can be coordinated.

Problem Statement

Solar PV is variable, while electricity demand and grid conditions change simultaneously. The challenge is to coordinate available renewable resources—Wind, Hydro and Solar PV—together with energy storage and conventional/base-grid support. The objective is to coordinate these resources so that renewable electricity can meet demand reliably while maintaining practical operating constraints, reducing the carbon footprint and conventional grid dependence, and moving toward a high-renewable, low-carbon electricity supply.

Project Objective

  • Model a hybrid Hydro–PV–Wind–BESS system.
  • Coordinate renewable generation and battery dispatch.
  • Use hydropower as a flexible balancing resource.
  • Minimize fossil-energy fallback in the simulated scenario.
  • Create a foundation for AI-enabled predictive energy management.
